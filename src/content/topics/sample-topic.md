## Introduction

Social media platforms such as Facebook, Twitter, Instagram, and WhatsApp have become an integral part of student life. The rapid growth of these platforms has raised concerns among educators and parents about their potential impact on academic performance. Students spend considerable time on these platforms, which could distract them from their studies.

## Objectives of the Study

The main objective of this study is to examine the effect of social media on academic performance. Specifically, the study aims to:

1. Determine the extent to which students use social media platforms daily
2. Examine the relationship between social media usage and academic performance
3. Identify the specific social media platforms most used by university students
4. Determine whether social media has a positive or negative effect on study habits

## Research Questions

The following research questions guided this study:

1. What is the extent of social media usage among university students?
2. Is there a significant relationship between social media usage and academic performance?
3. Which social media platforms are most frequently used by university students?
4. How does social media usage affect the study habits of students?

## Hypotheses

**H01:** There is no significant relationship between social media usage and the academic performance of university students.

**H02:** There is no significant difference in academic performance between heavy social media users and light social media users.

## Significance of the Study

This study will be beneficial to students, teachers, parents, and policymakers in understanding the impact of social media on academic performance.
