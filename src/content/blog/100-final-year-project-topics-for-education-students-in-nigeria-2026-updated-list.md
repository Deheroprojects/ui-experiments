100 Final Year Project Topics for Education Students in Nigeria (2026 Updated List)

Choosing a suitable project topic is one of the most important decisions every final-year student must make. A good research topic not only determines the quality of the study but also influences the ease of data collection, literature review, and project defense.

Education remains one of the most researched disciplines in Nigerian universities due to its direct impact on teaching, learning, policy development, and national growth. To assist students, we have compiled a list of current and researchable project topics suitable for undergraduate and postgraduate students in Education-related fields.

Why Choosing the Right Project Topic Matters

A good project topic should:

Address a relevant educational issue.
Be researchable within available resources.
Have sufficient literature for review.
Contribute to knowledge in the field.
Align with the student's area of interest.
